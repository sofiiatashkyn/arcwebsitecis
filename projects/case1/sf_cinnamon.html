Cinnamon Roll

by Tashkyn

Soft, fluffy rolls filled with cinnamon and brown sugar, topped with sweet cream cheese frosting (8-10 servings).

Rate/Review | Review Page

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour (375 g)
  • 3/4 cup warm milk (180 ml)
  • 1/4 cup sugar (50 g)
  • 1 packet yeast (7 g)
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup melted butter (57 g)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/3 cup softened butter (75 g)
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar (100 g)
  • 1.5 tablespoons cinnamon
  • 4 oz cream cheese (115 g)
  • 2 tablespoons butter (28 g)
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar (60 g)
  • 1-2 tablespoons milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Mix warm milk, sugar and yeast. Let sit for 5-10 minutes.
  2. Add flour, egg, melted butter, and salt. Mix and knead into dough.
  3. Cover the dough and let it rise for 1 hour.
  4. Mix softened butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon.
  5. Roll out the dough, spread the filling, and roll it up.
  6. Cut into 8-10 rolls and place in a baking dish.
  7. Bake at 350°F for 20-25 minutes.
  8. Mix cream cheese, butter, powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla to make the frosting.
  9. Spread the frosting over the warm cinnamon rolls and serve.
